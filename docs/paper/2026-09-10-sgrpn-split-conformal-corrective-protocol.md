## Fixed predictor and calibration

For each of five outer folds and seeds `20260723`, `20260724`, and `20260725`:

1. Fit the unchanged G1 mean path using only proper-training groups.
2. Fit both unchanged scale variants, `heteroscedastic` and `homoscedastic`, using only proper-training groups. Existing inner group splits may be used for epoch selection inside this role.
3. Lock the mean model, scale model, and all fitted scalers.
4. Predict every reserved calibration group with that locked predictor.
5. For scale model \(v\), define one score for calibration group \(g\):

   \[
   S_g^{(v)}=\max_{i\in g,\ r\in\{1,2,3\}}
   \frac{|y_{ir}-\hat\mu_i|}{\hat\sigma_i^{(v)}}.
   \]

6. For \(m=43\) calibration groups and \(\alpha\in\{0.10,0.05\}\), use the \(\lceil(m+1)(1-\alpha)\rceil\)-th ordered score without interpolation.
7. Apply that quantile to outer-test predictions generated by the same locked model and scaler checkpoints.

Calibration labels must not affect fitting, epoch selection, scaler estimation, or model choice. Outer-test labels are accessed only after model fitting and calibration quantiles are complete.

## Evaluation and stopping rules

- Exactly 15 fold/seed units are required. A unit is reusable only if its completion marker, full artifact hash closure, split roles, calibration scores, quantiles, and interval arithmetic all validate.
- Both scale variants are always retained and reported. Neither may be removed or relabelled because of corrective outcomes.
- Formal corrective evaluation is run once after 15/15 validated units.
- Undercoverage, excessive width, adverse probabilistic scores, or unstable seed results must be reported as observed and must not trigger tuning, seed/fold deletion, or result-driven rerunning.
- The finite-sample statement is limited to a future exchangeable machining group with the observed-type region and three-repeat measurement structure. It is not a guarantee under material, tool, machine, process, or temporal shift.
- The observed outer-fold results are corrective empirical evidence on a previously analysed dataset, not untouched confirmatory evidence. The three seed estimates share the same 212 groups and must not be interpreted as 636 independent groups.
- Original Phase A/Phase B point-prediction results remain historical primary evidence. Mean results from the reduced proper-training path are reported separately as the cost of reserving an independent calibration set.

### Corrective claim rule frozen before evaluation

This rule was added after independent implementation review but before any corrective evaluation invocation or corrective result access.  It is correction-specific and must not be described as the original Phase B preregistration.

- For each retained scale variant, empirical simultaneous-group coverage is described as meeting the tested nominal targets only when its all-seed outer-fold estimate is at least `0.90` for the 90% interval and at least `0.95` for the 95% interval.
- Heteroscedasticity may be emphasized only if the heteroscedastic variant meets both empirical coverage targets **and** has a strictly lower all-seed conformal Winkler score than the homoscedastic variant at both 90% and 95%.
- If either condition fails, the manuscript must state the failed condition explicitly and must not foreground heteroscedastic superiority.
- Both scale variants and all folds/seeds remain reported regardless of the decision.  The group split-conformal construction remains methodologically retained; this does not convert the reused-dataset outer-fold estimates into untouched confirmatory evidence.
- No practical-width threshold is registered.  Width, NLL, CRPS, negative transfer, and seed spread are descriptive and cannot trigger tuning or rerunning.
